The brew, start to finish

Four stages, every cup.

This is the same order we follow behind the bar and the order we recommend at home. Skip a stage and the cup tells on you.

  1. 01

    Bloom

    A small pour wakes the grounds and releases trapped CO₂ before the real extraction begins.

    0:00 – 0:45
  2. 02

    Pour

    Slow, concentric circles keep the bed saturated evenly, avoiding channels that drain too fast.

    0:45 – 2:00
  3. 03

    Drip

    Gravity finishes the job. We let the last of the water clear the bed on its own timeline.

    2:00 – 3:15
  4. 04

    Rest

    Thirty seconds off the burner lets the aromatics settle before the first sip.

    3:15 – 3:45
